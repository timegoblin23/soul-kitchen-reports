03 · What the data says

Four readings.

These are observations from the account data, not recommendations. The next step is to decide which pattern is worth testing.

1

Spend was concentrated in four months.

January to April 2026 accounted for £131,626, or 70.7% of the year's spend. January alone spent £37,092 and produced 3,772 purchases, the highest monthly purchase count in the window.

2

Efficiency stepped down through the spring.

ROAS moved from 2.27x in January to 1.54x in May, even as monthly spend was cut from £37.1k to £8.9k. The 2025 campaign stopped delivering on 26 May. The return fell even as spend came down, so this was not simply a volume problem.

3

September was a different machine.

The relaunch spent £14,940 in eight delivery days and returned 7.38x on 4,271 purchases. Link CPC was £0.35 against £0.86 on the 2025 campaign, and link CTR was 3.47% against 1.86% for the 12-month account. Its funnel mix is not like-for-like: checkout rate fell to 17.6% of landing views while conversion rose to 15.2%.

4

Order value moved up while volume moved down.

AOV rose from £18.28 in October to £25.80 in September, a 41% increase. Monthly purchases fell from 3,772 in January to 580 in May before the September relaunch. The account ended the year buying fewer, larger orders.

04 · Definitions

How each number is built.

Meta event counts are not strictly sequential — checkouts can exceed adds to cart in some months — so every funnel rate is divided by landing page views.

CTR (all)

All clicks ÷ impressions. Meta's default click metric includes clicks that do not leave for the website.

Link CTR

Link clicks ÷ impressions. The cleaner measure of traffic quality.

CPM

Spend ÷ impressions × 1,000. The cost of 1,000 impressions.

CPC (all) / CPC (link)

Spend ÷ all clicks, and spend ÷ link clicks. Link CPC is the cost of a visit.

LPV rate

Landing page views ÷ link clicks. How many clicks survive to a loaded page.

ATC rate

Adds to cart ÷ landing page views.

Checkout rate

Checkouts initiated ÷ landing page views.

Conversion rate

Purchases ÷ landing page views. The overall store conversion from paid traffic.

AOV

Attributed revenue ÷ purchases. Revenue is derived from Meta purchase ROAS × spend.

ROAS

Meta-attributed purchase revenue ÷ spend. Blended across campaigns in the monthly table.

Revenue attribution

Meta-reported purchase value, not the store's banked revenue. It reflects the attribution window and event matching at the time of reporting.

Data window

1 Oct 2025 – 30 Sep 2026. First delivery was 8 Oct 2025; June–August 2026 had no spend. Pulled 18 Oct 2026. Account ROAS is Meta's aggregate; campaign revenue is summed from daily ROAS, so the two can differ by under 0.1%.
