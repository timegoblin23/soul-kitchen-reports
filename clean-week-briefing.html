The next play: strategic roadmap

Now that we have successfully achieved scale and stability, our next phase is not to push budget further right now. In a rising CPM environment, increasing budget now just burns efficiency.

Instead, we enter the “Buy Information” phase. We use our spend to test new creative angles in the market to answer two questions:

  1. Does this message resonate and gain market traction?
  2. If it wins, does it unlock a brand new scale pocket for us to push into?
